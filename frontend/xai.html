Neural Network Attention Mapping (Grad-CAM)
MobileNetV2 Layer Attention

For rice leaf disease image classification, Grad-CAM (Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping) visualizes the exact spatial regions of the leaf that influenced the neural network's final classification layer.

Why Grad-CAM Matters:
It verifies that the model makes predictions by focusing on pathological lesions, necrotic margins, and discoloration patches on the leaf itself rather than background soil, sunlight glare, or image artifacts.
