Scientific Interpretation & Methodology
Level-2A Bottom-of-Atmosphere (BOA)

Scientific Description: NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) is calculated from red (B04: 665 nm) and near-infrared (B08: 842 nm) satellite reflectance. It provides an indicator of vegetation presence and vigor. NDVI is derived from satellite observations and should be interpreted together with crop type, growth stage, weather, and field conditions.

Important: NDVI is satellite-derived and is not a direct measurement of crop health.

Formula: NDVI = (B08 - B04) / (B08 + B04)
Spatial Sampling: 9-point median spatial buffer
Sensor: Sentinel-2 MultiSpectral Instrument (MSI)
